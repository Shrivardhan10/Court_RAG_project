The ratio laid down in the judgements o r the law declared by this Court do provide the guidelines to the judicial mind of the courts to decide the cases on hand, but the courts while applying the l aw also have to consider the evidence before them and the s urrounding circumstances under which the alleged offences are committed by the accused.
