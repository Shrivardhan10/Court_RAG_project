389 DELHI DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY V. NARVADA DEVI & ORS. (Civil Appeal No. 735 of 2023) FEBRUARY 09, 2023 [M. R. SHAH AND C. T. RAVIKUMAR, JJ.] Land Acquisition – Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 – s.24(2) – Respondent no.1 filed writ petition , claiming relief for declaration that acquisition proceedings pertaining to subject lands were deemed to have lapsed in view of s.24(2) of the Act, 2013 as neither physical possession of the subj ect lands had been taken nor compensation had been tendered to respondent no.1 – High Court allowed the writ petition and declared that acqui sition with respect to the land in question was deemed to ha ve lapsed – Held: The view taken by the High Court is contrary to the Constitution Bench decision of Supreme Court in the Indore Development Authority case – High Court was conscio us of the fact that the actual physical possession was not ca pable of being taken due to illegal occupation by the encroachers – Though, Hig h Court granted declaration that acquisition proceedings with respect to the subject lands were deemed to have lapsed, the High Court observed that respondent no.1 would only be entitle d to compensation as per Act, 2013 – Once it is held that respondent no.1 is not entitled to the relief of declaration o f lapsing of the acquisition proceedings, there is no question of pa yment of any compensation to him as per the Act, 2013 – Under the circum stances also, the impugned judgment of the High Court is uns ustainable.
