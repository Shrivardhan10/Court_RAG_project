We would reiterate that the process of criminal law c annot be utilised for arm-twisting and money recovery, parti cularly while opposing the prayer for bail. The question as to whether pre-arrest b ail, or for that matter regular bail, in a given case is to be granted or n ot is required to be examined and the discretion is required to be exercised by th e Court with reference to the material on record and the parameters g overning bail considerations. Putting it in other words, in a given case, the concession of pre-arrest bail or regular bail could be decli ned even if the accused has made payment of the money involved or offers to make any payment; conversely, in a given case, the concession of pre -arrest bail or regular bail could be granted irrespective of a ny payment or any offer of payment.
